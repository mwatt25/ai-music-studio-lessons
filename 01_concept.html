ChatGPT Prompt: Write My Lyrics
Here is my Song Blueprint: [Paste your Blueprint above] Using everything in this Blueprint, write complete song lyrics for me. Structure the song with: - [Verse 1] (4-6 lines) - [Chorus] (4-6 lines, the emotional hook) - [Verse 2] (4-6 lines, a different angle on the same story) - [Chorus] (repeat) - [Bridge] (2-4 lines, a shift in perspective or emotion) - [Final Chorus] (repeat, can vary slightly) Rules: - Use the specific details, phrases, and memories from the Blueprint - Do not use generic filler lines that could apply to anyone - Match the emotional tone I described - Avoid the things I said I do not want - Write in first person unless the Blueprint says otherwise

Optional help
Help With a Line

Copy the line you are stuck on, then paste it into ChatGPT with this:

"Here is a line from a song I am writing: [YOUR LINE]. Help me make it more specific and emotionally honest without changing the meaning."
Find a Rhyme

Paste this into ChatGPT:

"I need a word or phrase that rhymes with [YOUR WORD] and fits this emotional tone: [DESCRIBE THE FEELING]. Give me 5 to 10 options."
Help With Structure

Paste this into ChatGPT:

"Here are my lyrics so far: [YOUR LYRICS]. Help me organize them into a song structure with [Verse 1], [Chorus], [Verse 2], [Bridge], [Final Chorus]. Keep my exact words wherever they work. Only fill in what is genuinely missing."
Brainstorm a Hook

Paste this into ChatGPT:

"Here is what my song is about: [DESCRIBE YOUR SONG]. Give me 5 possible hook lines — the one memorable phrase that captures the whole emotional idea. Keep them specific to my story, not generic."
Get Feedback

Paste this into ChatGPT:

"Here are my lyrics: [YOUR LYRICS]. Without rewriting anything, tell me: What is working well? What feels incomplete? What might be missing for this to feel like a complete song?"
