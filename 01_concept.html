ChatGPT Prompt: Song Blueprint
I am creating a song using AI tools and I need your help building a Song Blueprint before I write any lyrics. Here is my raw material: - Who or what the song is about: [YOUR ANSWER] - Key memory or detail: [YOUR ANSWER] - Emotional tone I want: [YOUR ANSWER] - Mood: [YOUR ANSWER] - Genre: [YOUR ANSWER] - What to avoid: [YOUR ANSWER] - Any specific phrases or references: [YOUR ANSWER] Please create a Song Blueprint that includes: 1. A working title 2. The core emotional message (1 to 3 sentences) 3. Song structure using Verse 1, Chorus, Verse 2, Bridge, Chorus 4. A brief description of what each section should accomplish 5. Three possible hook and title options 6. Key words and phrases to use throughout
