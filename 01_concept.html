Why This Matters

AI can make a song, but it cannot know what makes your song worth hearing unless you supply meaningful raw material. The quality of what comes out depends entirely on the quality of what you put in.

Weak Starting Idea

"Make a love song for my husband."

ChatGPT has nothing real to work with. The result will be generic.

Strong Raw Material

How long you have been together, how you met, a recurring habit, a funny memory, the emotional tone you want, and what the song should avoid.

Song Structure Basics
▶
Video Demo
Demo video: Understanding song structure — coming soon
Teaching Point

You do not need music theory. You just need to know what the pieces of a song are called so you can tell ChatGPT and Suno what to build.

Verse

Tells the story. Sets the scene. Each verse usually covers different details.

Chorus

The emotional core. Repeated. This is the part people remember and sing along to.

Bridge

A shift in perspective or emotion. Creates contrast before the final chorus.

Hook

A short, catchy phrase, often the title, that anchors the whole piece.

Teaching Point

This prompt takes everything from your Story Bank and turns it into a structured Song Blueprint. That document is what you will use in every session that follows.

ChatGPT Prompt: Song Blueprint
I am creating a song using AI tools and I need your help building a Song Blueprint before I write any lyrics. Here is my raw material: - Who or what the song is about: [YOUR ANSWER] - Key memory or detail: [YOUR ANSWER] - Emotional tone I want: [YOUR ANSWER] - Genre: [YOUR ANSWER] - Mood: [YOUR ANSWER] - What to avoid: [YOUR ANSWER] - Any specific phrases or references: [YOUR ANSWER] Please create a Song Blueprint that includes: 1. A working title 2. The core emotional message (1 to 2 sentences) 3. A suggested song structure (Verse 1, Chorus, Verse 2, Bridge, Chorus) 4. A brief description of what each section should accomplish 5. Three possible hook and title options 6. Key words and phrases to use throughout
Open ChatGPT

Review Your Blueprint
Teaching Point

Before you move on, read your Blueprint out loud. Does it sound like your story? If something feels off, tell ChatGPT what to adjust. This is your foundation. It is worth getting right.
