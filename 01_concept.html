I am creating a song using AI tools and I need your help building a Song Blueprint before I write any lyrics. Here is my raw material: - Who or what the song is about: [fill in above] - Why I am making it: [fill in above] - 2 to 3 details only I would know: [fill in above] - A memory, phrase, or moment that belongs in it: [fill in above] - How it should feel: [fill in above] - What to avoid: [fill in above] - Genre: [choose above] - Mood: [choose above] Please create a Song Blueprint that includes: 1. A working title 2. The core emotional message (1 to 3 sentences) 3. Song structure: Verse 1, Chorus, Verse 2, Bridge, Chorus 4. A brief description of what each section should accomplish 5. Three possible hook and title options 6. Key words and phrases to use throughout
