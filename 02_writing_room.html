Structure the song with: - Verse 1 (4-6 lines) - Chorus (4-6 lines, the emotional hook) - Verse 2 (4-6 lines, a different angle on the same story) - Chorus (repeat) - Bridge (2-4 lines, a shift in perspective or emotion) - Final Chorus (repeat, can vary slightly) Label each section clearly: [Verse 1], [Chorus], [Verse 2], [Bridge], [Final Chorus] Rules: - Use the specific details, phrases, and memories from the Blueprint - Do not use generic filler lines that could apply to anyone - Match the emotional tone I described - Avoid the things I said I do not want - Write in first person unless the Blueprint says otherwise

Tag Guide
That's Me — specific, personal, only this song
Almost — close, but could be more specific
AI Alert — sounds like AI wrote it, not you
Doesn't Fit — wrong tone or direction for this song
Cut It — remove this line entirely
Lyrics Check
