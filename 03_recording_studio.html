Lesson 3A
How Suno Works
▶
Video Demo
Demo video: Suno interface overview — coming soon
Teaching Point

Suno generates complete songs with vocals, instruments, and production from a text prompt and optional lyrics. It works best when you give it a clear style description AND formatted lyrics with section tags.

Style Prompt

Describes the sound: genre, mood, tempo, vocal style, instrumentation. This is separate from your lyrics.

Lyrics Input

Your actual words, formatted with [Verse 1], [Chorus], [Bridge] tags so Suno knows the structure.

Custom Mode

Use Custom Mode in Suno to input both your style prompt AND your lyrics separately for maximum control.

Lesson 3B
Build Your Suno Style Prompt
▶
Video Demo
Demo video: Building a Suno style prompt in ChatGPT — coming soon
Teaching Point

Your style prompt is not a sentence. It is a comma-separated list of descriptors. Think of it as the production brief for your song's sound.

ChatGPT Prompt: Generate Suno Style Prompt
I need a Suno style prompt for my song. Suno style prompts are comma-separated lists of descriptors, not sentences. My song details: - Genre: [YOUR GENRE] - Mood: [YOUR MOOD] - Vocal style I want: [e.g., warm female vocals, soulful male voice] - Tempo feel: [e.g., mid-tempo, slow ballad, upbeat] - Instruments I want featured: [e.g., piano, acoustic guitar, strings] - Reference artists (optional): [e.g., sounds like early Beyonce] Generate a Suno style prompt that is 15 to 25 words, comma-separated, specific and evocative. Do not use sentences.
Example Style Prompts
R&B Love Song

soulful R&B, warm female vocals, mid-tempo, piano-led, lush strings, intimate production, emotional, cinematic, polished

Upbeat Celebration

upbeat pop, bright female vocals, celebratory, acoustic guitar, clapping percussion, radio-ready, joyful, summer feel

Lesson 3C
Format Your Lyrics for Suno
▶
Video Demo
Demo video: Formatting lyrics with section tags — coming soon
Teaching Point

Suno reads section tags to understand song structure. Always format your lyrics with brackets before pasting them in.

Correct Format

[Verse 1]
Your verse lyrics here...

[Chorus]
Your chorus lyrics here...

[Verse 2]
Your second verse here...

[Bridge]
Your bridge here...

[Chorus]
Final chorus here...

ChatGPT Prompt: Format Lyrics for Suno
Please reformat my lyrics for Suno. Each section needs a bracket tag like [Verse 1], [Chorus], [Bridge], [Outro]. Here are my lyrics: [PASTE YOUR LYRICS] Format rules: - Add the correct bracket tag before each section - Add a blank line between sections - Do not change any of the words - If a section repeats, label it [Chorus] each time

Always generate at least 2 versions. Suno is probabilistic. The same prompt can produce very different results. Your job is to listen, compare, and pick the one that serves your song.

3E
Lesson 3E
Battle of the Songs
Teaching Point

Do not pick based on first impressions. Use this scorecard to evaluate each version objectively, then choose the winner.

Vocal Fit

Does the vocal style match the emotion of the song? Rate 1 to 5 for each version.

Lyric Clarity

Can you understand the words clearly? Are the key lines landing the way you intended?

Production Feel

Does the instrumentation match the mood from your Blueprint?

Emotional Impact

Does it make you feel something? Would you play this for the person it is about?

3F
Lesson 3F
If You Do Not Love It, Fix It
Teaching Point

If neither version is right, do not keep generating blindly. Diagnose the problem first, then adjust your prompt or lyrics accordingly.

Problem: Wrong Vocal Style

Adjust your style prompt. Add more specific vocal descriptors. Reference an artist whose voice matches what you want.

Problem: Lyrics Not Landing

Go back to ChatGPT. Ask it to rewrite the specific lines that feel weak. Then re-generate in Suno with the improved lyrics.
