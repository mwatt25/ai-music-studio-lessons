Before You Begin
No music or video experience required. If you can describe what you want, the tools can build it.
Bring an idea and be willing to experiment. The first version is never the final version. That is part of the process.
Pause. Make. Return. When you see a prompt or activity, stop and do it before moving on. Do not binge-watch. The making is the point.
