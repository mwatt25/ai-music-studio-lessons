The Decision
What are you doing with this?

All three are valid. There is no wrong answer here.

Keep It Personal
Keep it for yourself or share it privately with someone.
Share It
Post it, send it, or share your song or video without a streaming release.
Release It
Distribute your song to streaming platforms.
Keep It Personal
That is a complete and valid choice.

Not every song needs an audience. A song made for someone's birthday, a tribute to a person you love, a story only your family would understand — these are complete works. They do not need Spotify to be real.

You can always change your mind later. The song is yours. The decision is yours.

Share It
You made something worth sharing.

You can share your song or video without distributing it to streaming platforms. A few ways to do that:

  • Share your Suno link directly — anyone with the link can listen
  • Post your sample video to Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, or anywhere you share content
  • Send the song or video privately to the person it was made for

If your song was created on Suno's free plan, sharing it in a personal, non-commercial context is permitted. Monetizing or commercially distributing a free-plan song is not. If you want to monetize, see the Release It path instead.

Before You Release

Commercial rights matter here. Songs created on Suno's free plan are for personal, non-commercial use and cannot be monetized or commercially distributed.

Songs created while subscribed to an eligible paid Suno plan (Pro or Premier) receive commercial-use rights for that song. Upgrading after the fact generally does not retroactively apply to songs already generated on the free plan.

If you generated your song on the free plan and want to release it commercially, the cleanest path is to subscribe to an eligible plan and regenerate your final version before distributing.

Note: commercial-use rights from Suno do not guarantee copyright protection. Suno's own documentation notes this distinction.

Distribution
How streaming distribution works.

A distribution service takes your song and delivers it to Spotify, Apple Music, Amazon Music, YouTube Music, and dozens of other platforms. You keep your rights. They handle the delivery.

What Happens When You Distribute
01
Upload your audio file — typically an MP3 or WAV exported from Suno.
02
Upload your cover art — square format, at least 3000 x 3000 pixels. The prompt you built in Session 4 can help generate this.
03
Fill in your song details — title, artist name, genre, release date.
04
Choose your platforms — most distributors send to all major platforms by default.
05
Wait — delivery typically takes 3 to 7 days. Spotify and Apple Music have their own review timelines.
06
Your song goes live — you get a streaming link you can share anywhere.
