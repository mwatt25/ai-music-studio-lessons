Lesson 5A

Where Do You Want to Put It?

Teaching Point

Where you release your song determines how you package it. A song for Instagram Reels needs different assets than a song going to Spotify. Decide first, then prepare.

Social Media

Instagram, TikTok, YouTube Shorts. You need a 30 to 60 second clip with captions and a strong visual hook in the first 3 seconds.

Streaming Platforms

Spotify, Apple Music, Amazon. You need a full-length song, cover art, and a distributor. DistroKid is what we used for this creative experience's song.

Personal Gift

A private link, a downloaded file, or a printed QR code. No platform needed. Just a way to share it directly.

YouTube

Full video upload. You need the complete music video, a title, description, and thumbnail. Great for long-term discoverability.

Teaching Point

A release package is everything someone needs to experience your song: the audio, the visual, the caption, and the context. Use ChatGPT to write the words. Use your existing assets for the rest.

ChatGPT Prompt: Social Media Caption
Write a social media caption for my AI-generated song. Song details: - Title: [TITLE] - Who or what it is about: [BRIEF DESCRIPTION] - Platform: [Instagram / TikTok / YouTube] - Tone: [Heartfelt / Funny / Proud / Celebratory] The caption should: - Open with a hook that stops the scroll - Tell the story of why I made this song in 2 to 3 sentences - End with a call to action (comment, share, tag someone) - Include 5 to 8 relevant hashtags at the end - Be authentic, not corporate or overly polished
Open ChatGPT
ChatGPT Prompt: Cover Art Description
I need cover art for my song to use on streaming platforms or social media. Song details: - Title: [TITLE] - Genre and mood: [GENRE AND MOOD] - Visual direction: [FROM SESSION 4] Generate a detailed image prompt I can use in an AI image generator (like DALL-E, Midjourney, or Adobe Firefly) to create cover art. The prompt should describe: composition, color palette, typography style, mood, and any specific visual elements. Make it feel like professional album artwork, not a generic AI image.
