The Decision
What are you doing with this?

Both are valid. There is no wrong answer here.

Keep It Personal

Keep It Personal

That is a complete and valid choice.

Not every song needs an audience. A song made for someone's birthday, a tribute to a person you love, a story only your family would understand — these are complete works. They do not need Spotify to be real.

You can always change your mind later. The song is yours. The decision is yours.

Before You Distribute

Commercial rights matter here. Songs created on Suno's free plan are for personal, non-commercial use and cannot be monetized or commercially distributed.

To release your song commercially, you need to have generated it while subscribed to an eligible paid Suno plan. Upgrading after the fact generally does not apply retroactively.

If you created your song on a free plan and want to release it, the cleanest path is to subscribe to an eligible Suno plan and regenerate your final version before distributing.

Distribution
How streaming distribution works.

A distribution service takes your song and delivers it to Spotify, Apple Music, Amazon Music, YouTube Music, and dozens of other platforms. You keep your rights. They handle the delivery.

What Happens When You Distribute
01
You upload your audio file — typically an MP3 or WAV from Suno.
02
You upload your cover art — square format, at least 3000 x 3000 pixels. The prompt you built in Session 4 can help you generate this.
03
You fill in your song details — title, artist name, genre, release date.
04
You choose your platforms — most distributors send to all major platforms by default.
05
You wait — delivery typically takes 3 to 7 days. Spotify and Apple Music have their own review timelines.
06
Your song goes live — you get a streaming link you can share anywhere.
