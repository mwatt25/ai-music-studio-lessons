What needs to change?
Vocals

In Suno, you can try a different vocal style by updating the Style field and regenerating. Try adding or changing the vocal feel descriptor (e.g. swap "Smooth" for "Raspy" or "Powerful"). If the pronunciation of a specific word is off, you can use Suno's Remix/Edit to adjust that section.
Energy or tempo

Update your Energy selection above, rebuild your Style description, and generate a new version. If the tempo is close but not quite right, try adding "slow tempo," "mid-tempo," or "uptempo" directly to your Style field in Suno.
Instrumentation or production

Update your Production Feel selection above and regenerate. You can also be more specific in Suno's Style field — for example: "acoustic guitar, no drums" or "piano-led, orchestral strings." The more specific, the better.
Intro or ending

Suno's Extend tool lets you add to the beginning or end of a song. For paid users, Replace Section lets you regenerate just the intro or outro while keeping the rest. Check your Suno plan for available editing tools.
A specific section

Suno's Replace Section (available on some plans) lets you select and regenerate a specific part of the song — a verse, chorus, or bridge — without changing the rest. If that is not available on your plan, try regenerating the full song with a more specific Style description.
Lyrics or pronunciation

Go back to Session 2 to revise your lyrics, then return here with the updated version. For pronunciation of a specific word, you can use Suno's Remix/Edit to adjust that section, or try adding phonetic spelling in brackets in the lyrics (e.g. [pro-nun-see-AY-shun]).
Something else

Update your Sound Board selections above to better match what you are hearing in your head, rebuild your Style description, and generate a new version. If you are not sure what to change, try generating 2 to 3 more versions with the same settings — Suno produces variations each time.
