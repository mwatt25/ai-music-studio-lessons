What needs to change?
Go back to Your Video Plan above, update the moment you want to change, and rebuild that clip's prompt. Generate a new clip in Google Flow and swap it in.
Go back to Pick Your World above, choose a different visual direction, and rebuild your clip prompts. The new style will update all three prompts automatically.
Go back to Pick Your Moment above, choose a different section of your song, and let the moments and prompts rebuild from there.
Try reordering your three clips in your video editor. Sometimes a different sequence changes the whole feel of the sample.
